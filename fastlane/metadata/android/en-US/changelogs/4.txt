A calmer, clearer Sober: the interface was rebuilt around a single accent colour, Inter and JetBrains Mono, and a stricter spacing grid.

- Every list row is now one screen-reader stop, with proper roles and labels.
- Emoji badges were replaced with a Material icon set that follows the theme.
- Contrast was raised across cards, secondary text and status colours.
- Tabs cross-fade instead of cutting, and motion is reduced when the system asks for it.
- The bottom bar gained a hairline edge above the content.
