Sober is a calm, on-device screen-time blocker for Android. It gives you a daily ceiling for each app, a ceiling for the whole phone, timed focus sessions, and a way to block the parts of an app that actually waste your time — Reels, Shorts, the For You feed — instead of the app itself.

Everything runs on your phone. There is no account, no cloud, no analytics, and the app does not request the internet permission at all. Nothing leaves the device.

FEATURES

Per-app daily limits
Set a limit for any installed app with a minute-by-minute drum picker. A seven-day bar chart shows what you actually used, and a switch pauses a limit without deleting it.

Overall daily cap
One budget for the whole phone, on top of individual limits. When it runs out, Sober blocks the apps you have not left out. Home, Settings, the keyboard and System UI are never blocked.

Focus sessions
Fifteen minutes to two hours, a custom duration, or open-ended. Choose the apps to block and optionally silence their notifications. Sessions survive screen-off and reboot through a foreground service, and ending one early starts a regret timer before the apps unlock.

Section blocking
Use Android's Accessibility API to close just the distracting section of an app: Instagram Reels and Explore, YouTube Shorts, a selected tab. Blocking a section never blocks the whole app.

Website blocking
Keep a domain closed inside the browser by reading its address bar. Chrome is verified; Chromium, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi, Firefox, Opera, DuckDuckGo, Samsung Internet and other browsers are covered best-effort. No VPN is used and no traffic is inspected or sent anywhere. In-app browsers and Custom Tabs cannot be read — block the host app instead.

Custom rules and schedules
Keep a named app or website closed, or mute notifications that contain a word you choose, at any hour. Schedules block chosen apps during set hours on set days.

Statistics
Today, 7 days or 30 days: total screen time, top apps, streaks, focus sessions and a heatmap of when you use the phone. An unobserved day is never counted as a success.

REQUIRED PERMISSIONS

Usage access — counts app usage.
Display over other apps — shows the block screen on top of an app.
Notifications — shows the ongoing status and the daily-cap countdown.
Accessibility (optional) — section blocking, website blocking and instant app-level blocks.
Notification listener (optional) — silences notifications during a focus session.

All of these are used locally. Sober has no internet permission, so none of them can be used to send data anywhere.
